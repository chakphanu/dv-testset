# M17 reflector link: obligations of the reflector side

What a reflector owes an M17 network client on UDP 17000: the control
packets (`CONN`, `LSTN`, `ACKN`, `NACK`, `PING`, `PONG`, `DISC`), the
54-byte stream packet (`M17 `) and the packet-mode packet (`M17P`). The
clients that talk to a reflector are M17Gateway (g4klx and the M17
Project fork; Pi-Star and WPSD hotspots run it), DroidStar and mvoice.
Each item is numbered `P-M17-NN`; the testlist rows are at the end of
this document. Stage 1 is the client side of the reflector, rows
P-M17-01..45 (sections 1-6); stage 2 is the reflector peer side and packet
mode, rows P-M17-46..86 (section 7). 86 rows in all: 45 + 41 obligations, judged by
39 cases in `cases/proto/test_m17.py`, the B-cases it refers to, and 60 cases
in `cases/proto/test_m17_peer_link.py` (17), `test_m17_relay.py` (21) and
`test_m17_packet.py` (22).

Every row says where the obligation comes from, in separate columns: the
spec text (document, section), what the clients send, expect and
tolerate, what the other reflectors do (project at a commit, behaviour in
our own words, no code copied), and what xlxd-ref did on the rig
(`measured:`, 2026-10-30, run `out/20261030T062259Z-i3`: 58 passed, 1
skipped, sut == ref everywhere; `--capture` dvcheck 0 errors). Rows judged
by existing B cases keep the code reading (`code:`). The stage 2 rows have no
xlxd-ref reading (`n/m`: xlxd has no M17 peering and drops M17P); mrefd is
their reference (section 7).

**Level**: MUST / SHOULD only where a spec says so (Part II uses "will",
"must" and "should" in plain English; the word is quoted);
`convention (impl)` where only implementations back it, naming the ones
that agree; `disputed` where reflectors differ, naming who does what.
Recommendation priority: spec + client consensus > other reflectors >
xlxd.

## How the cases judge

`cases/proto/test_m17.py` probes xlxd-ref and the system under test in
parallel, as `test_mmdvm.py` does:

- **hb**: a spec obligation ("will", "must") that xlxd also meets;
  asserted on both sides.
- **diff**: a convention the reflectors agree on; sut == ref.
- **dev**: reflectors disagree or xlxd deviates from the spec / the
  clients: under `DVT_SUT_PROFILE=xlxd` sut == ref (xlxd's behaviour is
  recorded), under another profile `SPEC["m17"]["P-M17-NN"]` decides,
  skip "spec pending" while there is none.
- **spec (Bn)**: judged by the DV-Router behaviour rows (testlist B).

Stage 2 (section 7) is judged against the decided DV-Router behaviour with
mrefd as the calibration side; its rules are in 7.1.

With `DVT_REF_KIND=urfd` (URFd as the reference, compatibility data)
the relay probes address `XLX999 <module>` instead of broadcast, because
URFd drops any destination that is not a callsign with the module at
position 8 (P-M17-24); P-M17-24/36 still probe every form.

The tester's stream packets carry a zero CRC and broadcast destination
unless a case sets them (dvrouter `NOT_CHECKED` lists "M17 LSF CRC");
cases that need a valid CRC compute it ([M17-1] 2.6).

Layout used below (54 B stream packet, [M17-2] "Stream Mode Packets"): 0
`M17 `, 4 stream id, 6 DST, 12 SRC, 18 TYPE (2), 20 META (14), 34 FN
(bit 15 = last frame), 36 payload (two 8-byte Codec2 3200 frames, or one
Codec2 1600 frame + 8 data bytes), 52 CRC over bytes 0-51.

### 7.1 Rig topology, fake peers, how the cases judge

The tester plays two interlink peers of the reflector under test:

| Peer | Designation | Port | Modules shared with the node | Tester address |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M17-DVT | `M17-DVT` | UDP 17011 (`DVT_M17_PEER_DVT_PORT`) | `M` (`DVT_M17_PEER_DVT_MODULES`) | `<net>.30` |
| M17-DVU | `M17-DVU` | UDP 17012 (`DVT_M17_PEER_DVU_PORT`) | `MN` (`DVT_M17_PEER_DVU_MODULES`) | `<net>.30` for the sut, `<net>.32` for mrefd |

So module M is shared by both peers and N only by M17-DVU. The cases put
local clients on M (talker, listener, one listen-only `LSTN`) and on N, and
derive every expected receiver from these sets (a datagram from a client
goes to the clients of its module and to every peer sharing it; a peer's
datagram goes to the clients of its trailer's module and to no peer).

SUT configuration (DV-Router native config, same sha as the build under
test): `edges.m17.designation = "M17-DVR"` is the SUT's own designation (the
setting is required when peers are configured, pattern `^M17-[A-Z0-9]{3}$`),
and one `edges.m17.peers` row per peer with `callsign`, `host`, `port` and
`modules`: `M17-DVT` at the tester on 17011 with `M`, `M17-DVU` at the
tester on 17012 with `MN`. The designation expected by the cases is
`SPEC["m17"]["designation"]` in `dvtest/specs/dvrouter.py` and is used in
every expectation (CONN, ACKN, PING, PONG, DISC).

Reference side (rig profile `mrefd`, `DVT_REF_KIND=mrefd`): mrefd with
designation `M17-REF`, modules `MNOP`, and the same two interlink peers. mrefd
keys peers by IP address only (its address compare ignores the port), so the
M17-DVU peer sits on its own tester address (`<net>.32`, added by the
`tester-addr` sidecar) or a DISC or PING of one peer would remove or
refresh the other. The sut keys by ip:port and keeps `<net>.30`. See
`docs/rig.md` ("mrefd as an extra reference") and `docs/compat-mrefd.md`.

Fake peer behaviour (`dvtest/m17_peer.py`, fixture `m17_peers`): it binds
its configured port and sends every datagram from it; it answers a
reflector `CONN` with a 37 B `ACKN` (its own designation and module list),
`ACKN`s the reflector's own `CONN`, sends its own `CONN` too, sends `PING`
every 3 s once linked and never answers a `PING`. Datagrams to the
reflector are 55 B streams and n+1 B `M17P` with a valid module trailer
unless a case builds them bare on purpose. Link-up is seen by the sut's
first `PING` (3 s after the link) or by a relayed datagram.

Judging rule. The sut is asserted against the DV-Router decisions in
`dvtest/specs/dvrouter.py` (7.2, `Qn`); a row marked `dev` has its values
in `SPEC["m17"]["P-M17-NN"]` (skip "spec pending" while there is none) and a
row marked `record` prints and asserts nothing. mrefd is the calibration
side: the same probe runs on it and it is asserted where it agrees with the
decision; where it differs (reconnect timing, NACK vs silence, module order,
`URF`/`XLX` destination rewrite, bare `M17P` last-byte strip, encrypted
`M17P`, 860 B) only the sut is asserted and mrefd is printed (7.10). xlxd
has no M17 peering, so under `DVT_SUT_PROFILE=xlxd` these cases skip.
`DVT_SUT_LAUNCH=import` skips them too: the legacy importer has no M17
interlink input, so run them in a second pass with the native-config
launch on the same sha. Streams that are expected to stay silent are
observed for 3 s; every case prints what each side did.

Calibration note. mrefd overflows by one byte on an 860 B client `M17P`
(one over the 859 B a client may send): it accepts and relays it and a peer
then gets 861 B. That is mrefd-specific and the case set sends exactly one
860 B packet (P-M17-84).
The first group 1 and 3 calibration runs (2026-10-31) were made while the
M17-DVU module set was `NO`. The rig default is now `MN` (M shared by both
peers, N only by M17-DVU), and the final calibration on mrefd was run with
`MN`: all stage-2 cases pass on mrefd, the stage-1 cases that differ between
xlxd and mrefd are expected differences of a mixed run, not failures of this
suite. Measured values quoted below that name modules were taken with `NO`
or `MN` as stated in the row; the cases derive every expectation from the
peers' module sets.

## Client facts behind the recommendations

Each one is a row above; collected here with the client and commit:

- Stream CRC: only MV @ `c39f91e` sends a correct CRC (bytes 0-51). GW @
  `c72b989` and GWF @ `3035f2e` rewrite the destination without
  recomputing it; DS @ `4ef18be` computes it over the 28 LSD bytes only and
  sends 0x0000 in its end packet. A reflector that drops on a bad CRC
  silences them all (P-M17-25).
- DS @ `4ef18be` never answers PING; it sends PONG every 8 s on its own
  timer and has no link timeout (P-M17-10).
- Every client sends a 9th character in the CONN callsign: GW/GWF the
  Suffix (R or H), DS a fixed `D`, MV its client module (default `D`)
  (P-M17-02).
- DS @ `4ef18be` and MV @ `c39f91e` accept only a 4-byte ACKN (MV also only
  from the link address); GW/GWF match the first 4 bytes (P-M17-01).
- GW @ `c72b989` and GWF @ `3035f2e` overrun a 100-byte buffer on a longer
  `M17 ` datagram (P-M17-45).
- GW/GWF resend CONN every 3 s until ACKN and stop for good on NACK, so a
  NACK to a repeated CONN (MREFD) or silence to a bad one costs a link
  (P-M17-04, P-M17-07).

## xlxd-ref loop detection (not M17-specific)

xlxd @ `a978f03` blocks a callsign that sends three consecutive short
streams (under 50 codec frames, about 1 s) on one module with gaps under
2 s: a TX block of 3 s, then 10 s, then 300 s with the link refused
(CONN answered NACK, the client removed). M17 test streams are therefore
30 packets (60 frames). A first suite run with 24-packet streams tripped
it on xlxd-ref only (xlxd-sut, the PCM/USRP branch, did not), which shows
up as a NACK to a valid CONN. No spec covers it; DV-Router may choose its
own loop policy.
